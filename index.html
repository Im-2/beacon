The Problem and The Solution

Problem

Earnings on a schedule

Tokenized US stocks trade 7×24, but earnings and guidance still land in scheduled bursts. Human traders can't react instantly around the clock.

An agent that never sleeps

Beacon senses events the instant they land, has an LLM judge them, and executes with real risk controls — continuously, not on a schedule.

Solution

How Beacon Works

01

Sense

Beacon watches SEC filings and earnings data across a liquid stock universe, detecting 8-K and earnings events the moment they're filed.

02

Judge & Execute

An LLM evaluates each event's substance and guidance tone, then autonomously sizes and places a paper trade with defined risk parameters.

03

Log & Learn

Every decision — the data used, the reasoning, the outcome — is logged transparently for full explainability.

Risk controls the agent can't override

Deterministic checks — never left to the LLM's judgment.

Position size cap

  • Max 5% of paper capital per trade
  • Applies regardless of the agent's conviction score

Daily drawdown breaker

  • Halts new entries if daily loss hits 3%
  • Resumes automatically next session

Max concurrent positions

  • At most 5 open positions at once
  • Prevents overexposure across simultaneous triggers

No averaging into losers

  • Structurally blocked at the code level
  • Never delegated to the LLM's judgment
